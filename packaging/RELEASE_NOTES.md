## Download

**[CrateCut-mac.zip](https://github.com/dbajpeyi/scratch/releases/latest/download/CrateCut-mac.zip)** — VST3 + Audio Unit + standalone app, universal (Apple Silicon and Intel).

## Install

1. Unzip and open the `CrateCut` folder.
2. Double-click **Install CrateCut.command**.
3. **First time only:** macOS blocks it because it isn't notarised by Apple. Click *Done*, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Restart FL Studio, then **Options → Manage plugins → Find installed plugins**. CrateCut appears under *Generators*.

Everything installs for your user only. No admin password is needed.
