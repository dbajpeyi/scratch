CrateCut - installing on macOS
==============================

1. Double-click "Install CrateCut.command".

   The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the developer
   (CrateCut isn't notarised by Apple). That's expected:

     - Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to the message about "Install CrateCut.command"
       and click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

   A Terminal window opens, installs everything and tells you when
   it's done. You don't need to type anything.

2. Restart FL Studio, then Options > Manage plugins >
   Find installed plugins. CrateCut appears under Generators.

What gets installed (for your user only, no admin password):
   CrateCut.vst3       -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   CrateCut.component  -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   CrateCut.app        -> ~/Applications   (standalone version)

To uninstall, delete those three items.
(In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the folder path.)
